# Glossary

1. **Mutual fund** — Many investors' money managed together under an investment scheme.
2. **Scheme** — A specific mutual fund product with its own objective and NAV.
3. **Investor** — A person who invests in a mutual fund.
4. **Folio** — An account reference used to group an investor's mutual fund holdings.
5. **NAV (Net Asset Value)** — Per-unit value of a mutual fund scheme.
6. **Unit** — A share of ownership in a mutual fund scheme.
7. **Purchase** — An order to invest money and receive fund units.
8. **Redemption** — An order to sell fund units and receive money.
9. **Switch** — A request to redeem from one scheme and invest in another.
10. **SIP (Systematic Investment Plan)** — A plan to invest a set amount at regular intervals.
11. **Order** — An instruction from an investor to transact.
12. **Cut-off time** — The time used to decide which NAV date applies to an order.
13. **Funds realisation** — The time when the investment money is received and available.
14. **Business day** — A weekday that is not listed as a market holiday.
15. **Holiday calendar** — A list of dates when the market is closed.
16. **Investment lot** — Units acquired together on one purchase date, tracked as a group.
17. **Exit load** — A possible charge for redeeming units within a specified period.
18. **ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme)** — A tax-saving equity mutual fund category with a lock-in rule.
